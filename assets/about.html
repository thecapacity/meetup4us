Meetup4.us

Find a fair middle spot for your group.

How to use

  1. Add 2 or more locations: addresses or places. Tap 📍 in the search box to add where you are.
  2. The blue dot shows the midpoint, and the circle shows the area around it.
  3. Share the page link with your browser's share button. Everyone sees the same plan.
